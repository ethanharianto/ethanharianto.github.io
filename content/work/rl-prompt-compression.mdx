## The reward

TODO. The per-token KL reward, and the two ways it's wrong:

- **Keep/drop asymmetry.** The output space is asymmetric — dropping a
  needed token destroys the answer, keeping an unneeded one costs a
  little compute. A reward summed symmetrically doesn't see that.
- **Faithfulness mismatch.** Scoring against the ground-truth answer
  asks the compressed prompt to reproduce one known outcome, when it's
  actually being asked to preserve what the model would have concluded.
